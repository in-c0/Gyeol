# Moonshots

Moonshots run in parallel with mainline engineering. They are not product dependencies. A moonshot is merged only after evidence crosses a deliberately high threshold and the result improves the system objective vector.

## M1 — Semiconductor / compute

- **M1-H1:** Near-/in-sensor computation reduces sensory data movement by >90% for a defined perception workload.
- **M1-H2:** A new accelerator/device achieves >=10x lower joules per useful inference than the selected contemporary mobile-NPU baseline at equivalent task quality and latency.
- **M1-H3:** Useful computation can operate reliably in deformable device regions without relying entirely on rigid silicon islands.
- **M1-H4:** Nonvolatile compute materially lowers always-on standby energy for the personal-AI workload.

## M2 — Programmable / self-healing materials

- A thin structural material can transition between compliant and phone-rigid states with low energy, fast transition, and >=100k lifecycle.
- A user-replaceable or self-healing outer stack materially extends flexible-display service life.

## M3 — Flexible energy storage

- A conformal cell can meet practical safety, cycle-life, energy-density, and mechanical-fatigue requirements sufficiently to outperform segmented rigid cells at the system level.

## M4 — Ambient-pressure high-temperature superconductivity

This branch has an intentionally strict evidence ladder:

1. computationally plausible candidate near ambient pressure
2. independent theoretical reproduction
3. synthesis and structural characterization
4. reproducible zero-resistance transition
5. independent magnetic evidence consistent with superconductivity
6. independent laboratory replication

No intermediate step is described publicly as room-temperature superconductivity.

## M5 — Neuromorphic personal computer

- A sparse, event-driven architecture can provide materially better full-day contextual intelligence per joule than a conventional periodically sampled mobile architecture.
